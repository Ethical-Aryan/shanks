The Pirate's Cryptographic Code

AES-256-GCM (Galois/Counter Mode): Uses military-grade 256-bit symmetric encryption with a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 salted key derivation (100,000 iterations) and 96-bit random initialization vector (nonce). If any rival pirate alters even a single byte of the ciphertext, the GCM authentication tag rejects it instantly.
Aztec Caesar Shift: A historic rotation cipher derived from Julius Caesar and ancient nautical shift dials. Shifts letters along the alphabet by the secret key modulo 26, creating an encoded scroll readable only by those who hold the matching degree.
