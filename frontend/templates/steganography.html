How Pirate LSB Steganography Works

Least Significant Bit (LSB) Embedding: Every pixel on a screen consists of Red, Green, and Blue color values (0-255). We alter only the least significant bit (e.g., changing 184 to 185). The difference is completely invisible to human eyes, yet conceals thousands of binary characters.
Lossless PNG Preservation: Unlike JPEG which compresses and destroys pixel bits, PNG preserves every single bit losslessly. Combined with AES-256, even if an enemy pirate suspects steganography, they cannot read the message without the secret key.
